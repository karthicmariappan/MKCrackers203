# MK Crackers Mobile Responsive Final

- No horizontal page scrolling.
- Header collapses to a menu.
- Hero content stacks cleanly.
- Festival Offers and Gift Box cards fit side-by-side on phones.
- Search, category and sort controls stack vertically.
- Product price-list becomes individual product cards instead of a 1100px desktop table.
- Quantity controls remain touch-friendly.
- Cart becomes a single-column mobile layout.
- Customer form fields become full-width and touch-friendly.
